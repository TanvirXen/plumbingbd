How it works

WhatsApp message or form, an Order ID, a uniformed technician, cost agreed first, pay after — step by step.

Message

WhatsApp us the problem, a photo and your area.

We arrive

A uniformed technician comes at your chosen time.

Fixed

Everything is agreed before work starts. Pay after the job.

  • Two ways to order

    One-tap WhatsApp message (A), or a short form with work, address, date and time (B). No login.

  • An Order ID

    Every order gets an ID like ES-301026-1. Quote it whenever you contact us about that job.

  • Cost first, then work

    We price the job from photos or on site. Work starts only when you agree.

  • Pay after the job

    Once the work is completed: Cash, bKash, Nagad, NPSB bank transfer.
