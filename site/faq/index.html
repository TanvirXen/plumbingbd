Booking and timing

How do I book an electrician or plumber?

Three ways: tap "Order now" to send a WhatsApp message, fill in the short order form (work, address, date and time), or call 01609-993537. You don't need an account or an app.

What is the Order ID in my WhatsApp message?

Every order gets an ID like ES-301026-1 (service letters, job date, number). Keep it — quote it when you message us about that job, for a follow-up visit or a bill.

Are you really open 24 hours, including Friday and holidays?

Yes, we take orders 24 hours a day, every day, including Fridays and government holidays. At night we go first to urgent jobs: no power, sparking, short circuit, water leaking or overflowing. During Eid holidays please message first so we can confirm a time.

How fast can a technician reach me?

For urgent jobs in most of Uttara we usually arrive in about 60 minutes. Bashundhara and other areas can take longer depending on traffic. Booked jobs come at the time you choose.

Can I book for a specific date and time?

Yes. In the order form pick a day (up to a week ahead) and a 2-hour time slot, or choose Urgent. We confirm the time on WhatsApp.

Which areas do you cover?

All sectors of Uttara, all blocks of Bashundhara R/A, and nearby north Dhaka areas such as Uttarkhan, Dakkhinkhan, Diabari and Abdullahpur. The Areas page has the full list. Outside these areas, message us — we often still come.

Does someone need to be at home?

Yes, please keep an adult at home (or a contact person at the office or shop) to show us the problem and check the work before we leave.

Plumbing problems

Water is leaking into the flat downstairs — what do you do?

We find the exact source first — bathroom floor, pipe joint, commode or drain — then open only what's needed and fix it. We explain the options before any breaking.

My water pressure is low or the pump isn't lifting water — can you help?

Yes. We check the pump (motor), valves, pipes and tank line, then repair or install the right pump. The water pump calculator on this site helps you pick a size.

My commode, basin or kitchen drain is blocked — can you clear it without damage?

Yes. We clear blockages with the right tools, without breaking fittings, and tell you what caused it so it doesn't come back.

Do you install geysers and connect the water line?

Yes — geyser mounting, hot and cold water line connection and a leak check. Our electricians can also do the separate power line the geyser needs.

Do you fix leaks without breaking the whole bathroom?

We first find the exact leak point, then open only what's needed. We explain the options before any breaking.

Can you replace taps, showers, commodes and basins?

Yes — we install and replace all bathroom and kitchen fittings, including mixers, angle valves, washing machine and water purifier lines.

Cost and payment

Will I know the cost before work starts?

Yes. We look at the problem (on WhatsApp photos or on site), tell you the cost and agree it with you before starting. Nothing extra is added without asking you first.

Is there a visit or checkup charge?

Any electric or plumbing checkup is 350 Tk, and it can be reduced depending on the work. We confirm the amount on WhatsApp before we come.

Why are there no prices on the website?

Every job is different — cable length, pipe type, wall, height, parts. Send a photo or short video on WhatsApp and we give you a clear estimate first.

How do I pay — are bKash, Nagad or bank transfer accepted?

You pay after the work is completed, by Cash, bKash, Nagad, NPSB bank transfer.

Do I get a bill or memo?

Yes, on request we give a memo for home jobs. Offices and companies get a written quotation before work and a bill with the Order ID after.

Parts, safety and trust

Do you bring the parts, or should I buy them?

We bring common parts and can buy anything else nearby. You can also buy your own parts — we'll tell you exactly what to get. We use branded parts and show you the receipt.

Is there a warranty on your work?

Parts carry the maker's warranty. If the same problem comes back from our work, message us with your Order ID and we'll come and check it again.

Are your technicians our own staff? Is it safe to let them in?

Yes — our own trained team, in company uniform. We send the technician's name and phone number before the visit, and you can call our main number any time to check.

Can I talk to you in Bangla?

Of course. Message or call in Bangla or English — whatever is easier for you.

Do you do both electrical and plumbing work?

Yes. Emergency Electric and Plumbing Service has an Electrical Division (electricservicebd.com) and a Plumbing Division (plumbingservicebd.com), plus AC and home services. One message, one team.

Electrical problems

I smell burning or see sparks — what should I do?

Switch off the main breaker (MCB) at once, keep away from water and don't touch the burnt switch or wire. Then tap Order now and mark it Urgent, or call us — this is a priority job.

Why does my breaker (MCB) keep tripping?

Usually an overloaded line (AC, geyser or iron on the same circuit), a short circuit, a loose connection or moisture. We find the cause and fix it — never just fit a bigger breaker.

What size IPS do I need for load shedding?

It depends on the fans, lights, router and TV you want to run and for how long. Use the IPS load calculator on this site, or send us the list on WhatsApp and we'll suggest a size.

There's a problem with the electricity meter or the line outside — can you fix it?

The meter and the line up to it belong to the electricity company (DESCO in Uttara and Bashundhara) — contact them for those. We handle everything after the meter: the main board, breakers, wiring, sockets and appliances.

Do you do full wiring for a new flat or an old building?

Yes. We plan the load, use branded cables of the right size, separate lines for AC and geyser, proper earthing and a labelled distribution board.

Are your electricians trained for safe work?

Yes. The team is led by an electrical engineer. We switch off and test lines before working, use correctly rated cables and breakers, and check earthing.

Offices and businesses

Do you work for offices, shops and companies?

Yes — offices, shops, restaurants, super shops, clinics, schools and apartment buildings, from one-off repairs to full installations.

Can you work at night or on Friday so our business isn't disturbed?

Yes. We regularly work early morning, at night and on weekends so shops and offices keep running.

Do you offer a monthly maintenance contract?

Yes. Regular checks of boards, wiring, lighting and plumbing, priority response for faults and a monthly report. See the maintenance contract page or ask for a site survey.

Do you give a written quotation and bill for companies?

Yes. After a site survey we send a written quotation with the scope of work, and a bill after the job.
