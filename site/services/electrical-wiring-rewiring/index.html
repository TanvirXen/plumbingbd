How is a rewiring job estimated?

By flat size, number of points and cable brand. Send photos on WhatsApp or book a site visit for a written estimate.

When does a flat need rewiring?

If wiring is 20+ years old, cables heat up, breakers trip often, or you see burnt sockets.
